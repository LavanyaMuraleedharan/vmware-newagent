## 3. Final Cost Agent response

The analysis identified 674 underutilized VMware virtual machines, with an illustrative potential monthly savings of $25,000. This estimate is based on a configured VMware monthly demonstration estimate and is not derived from verified VM-level costs or utilization data. Therefore, it is considered an illustrative figure dependent on the assumptions provided. To accurately assess potential savings, validation of VM costs and utilization metrics is necessary, particularly focusing on workload peaks and actual resource consumption. It is important to verify whether these VMs are truly underutilized and if their workloads can be consolidated or decommissioned without impacting operational performance. This process should include workload validation, performance analysis, and confirmation of business requirements. The findings have been forwarded to the FinOps team for further financial evaluation, including detailed savings assessment, ROI, payback period, and risk analysis. No infrastructure changes have been executed or approved based on this preliminary assessment.
